NRAnimation studies / 01—11

A computational plate room

Eleven studies in living form.

Possible replacements for the four static plates. Each study is drawn live, has a different tempo, and responds subtly to the pointer. The shared palette keeps them inside the existing site rather than making them feel like embedded demos.

S.01 / FLOWLive system

Field Notes

Flow field
Warm phosphor

Thousands of short-lived traces follow an invisible vector field: part weather map, part pencil study, part machine dream.

S.02 / MOIRÉLive system

Signal Fold

Moiré study
Op-art

A stack of razor-thin waves slips in and out of alignment, producing a textile-like shimmer without gradients or imagery.

S.03 / ORBITLive system

Unstable Orbit

Harmonograph
Celestial

A slowly precessing family of curves with the poise of an astronomical instrument and the slight wrongness of impossible geometry.

S.04 / CHROMELive system

Liquid Index

Iridescent mesh
Y2K restraint

A soft, metallic membrane made from layered contours—your “shimmery stuff,” but disciplined to the site’s editorial grid.

S.05 / WAVELive system

Ghost Signal

Oscilloscope
CRT afterglow

A luminous signal keeps almost resolving into a symbol. The glow is analogue; the behaviour is mathematical.

S.06 / SWARMLive system

Soft Assembly

Particle swarm
Networked form

A porous organism continuously gathers and disperses—closest to the current network motif, but more atmospheric and alive.

S.07 / CONTOURLive system

Moving Isobar

Topographic lines
Slow weather

Nested pressure systems migrate across an imaginary territory: a cartographic texture with no fixed geography.

S.08 / BLOOMLive system

Magnetic Bloom

Polar geometry
Kinetic emblem

A radial instrument alternately flowers and collapses, sitting between a diffraction pattern, seal, and impossible mechanism.

S.09 / DITHERLive system

Dither Current

Halftone field
Digital patina

A deliberately low-resolution current catches the light like perforated metal—graphic, tactile, and quietly early-digital.

S.10 / NODESLive system

Resonant Matter

Chladni field
Acoustic geometry

Particles collect around the nodal lines of a continuously changing virtual plate, making sound structure visible without audio.

S.11 / WEAVELive system

Vector Weave

Ribbon system
Chromatic braid

Two mathematical fabrics pass through one another without touching, producing a compact field of depth and shifting metallic colour.

Direction: these sit somewhere between computational modernism, retrofuturist scientific graphics, early computer art, and ambient data sculpture.

Interaction: move across a specimen to disturb its field. “Regenerate” changes its internal parameters; “Pause motion” lets you compare still compositions.
