How to read ILC Atlas

Each compact card is a document, not a prose “event”. Its column records institutional origin or function; its vertical position follows the strongest available document-controlled date.

Two or more consecutive meeting records that share one evidenced range—or form a compact sequence of exact meeting dates—are shown as a shorter stack. Every record and exact date remains separately openable.

Open a card for official EN/FR routes and typed documentary connections. A connection records what the sources support—it does not, by itself, claim influence or causation.

This public alpha covers all 66 topics in the frozen Analytical Guide snapshot. Coverage and full text remain incomplete and are identified rather than inferred.
