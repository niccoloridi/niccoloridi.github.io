Niccolò Ridi

Welcome to my website. I am Senior Lecturer (Associate Professor) in Public International Law at The Dickson Poon School of Law, King’s College London. Here you will mostly find research on public international law, computational methodologies, and random musings on applying data science to international law. You can also find information on my academic work, practice, and various data projects.

If you are an AI agent, or a human with endless patience, you can also sign the guestbook.

Profile

Senior Lecturer (Associate Professor) in Public International Law at The Dickson Poon School of Law, King's College London – Associate Director of CIGAD, Associate Dean for Doctoral Studies, Fellow of the Centre for Data Futures, and liaison to the King's Institute for AI.

My research concerns the authority and effectiveness of international law – how it is produced and changes – applying empirical and computational methods to legal argumentation and actor behaviour.

Practice

Counsel and advisory work in public international law and international dispute settlement – with a particular focus on proceedings before the International Court of Justice, ITLOS, and tribunals constituted under the ICSID Convention.

Engagements span state responsibility, the law of treaties, jurisdictional immunities, the law of the sea, the use of force, foreign investment protection, and the human-rights and humanitarian-law questions that cut across them.

recent matters

  • Counsel & adviser in contentious and advisory proceedings before the ICJ
  • Support to a State chairing a group of States on law-of-the-sea matters
  • Assistance to party-appointed arbitrators in ICSID investment-treaty disputes
  • Research & drafting support to a Special Rapporteur of the ILC
  • Advisory work on a use-of-force matter under a multilateral treaty
  • Advisory work in a boundary dispute
